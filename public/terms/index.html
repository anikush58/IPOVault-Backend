2. Application & Allotment Tracking Limitations

No Financial Execution: IPOVault is an application recorder and status aggregator. IPOVault does not place bids on stock exchanges, does not process UPI mandate payments, does not block bank funds, and does not interact with Application Supported by Blocked Amount (ASBA) banking channels. All IPO bids must be submitted through your authorized broker, banking portal, or syndicate member.

Official Registrar Queries: When querying allotment status, the application queries public enquiry portals hosted by official SEBI-registered share transfer agents and registrars (such as Link Intime, KFin Technologies, Bigshare Services, Skyline Financial Services, and Purva Sharegistry). IPOVault acts as a convenience lookup client and does not control registrar data processing or release schedules.
