Account & Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy. Under the Google Play User Data Policy and applicable privacy laws, you can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion

Instant & Recommended

If you have the IPOVault app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open the IPOVault app.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon in the top header).
  3. Select Edit Profile.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the consequences and confirm the prompt.

Your server records, linked broker tokens, saved PANs, and local offline database will be immediately wiped.

Data Deletion Policy & Scope

When an account deletion request is confirmed, your personal data is processed according to the following policy:

Data Permanently Deleted

  • Authentication Identity: Firebase Auth credentials, email, and UID.
  • Tax & Identity Records: All encrypted and hashed PAN numbers.
  • Broker Connections: Demat links, OAuth access/refresh tokens, and credentials.
  • Portfolio & Bids: IPO applications, allotment tracker records, and trades.
  • Notifications: Push tokens, delivery history, and preference settings.
  • Cloud Profiles: Avatar photos, usernames, and cloud synchronization profiles.

Data Retained (Operational Audit)

  • Operational Tier Records: Subscription and entitlement tier change events are retained for dispute resolution and accounting reconciliation.
  • Complete Anonymization: All user IDs, IP addresses, and personal associations are permanently replaced with an isolated random surrogate ID.
  • Zero PII: No retained record can be linked back to you, your devices, or your personal identity.
Completion Timeframe: Immediate upon email confirmation link click. Requests submitted via the web verification portal are finalized automatically or within a maximum of 48 hours.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (App Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, submit your registered account email below. We will send a secure, single-use confirmation link to verify your identity.

For your protection, we never ask for passwords, PANs, or broker credentials. A confirmation link valid for 1 hour will be delivered to your inbox.
